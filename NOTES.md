# My notes on Frank

My notes now live inside **[Frank's Field Guide](field-guide.html)**, next to the
explanations they respond to. Look for the dashed boxes marked *My note*.

The guide is a study guide I made with Claude to break down how Frank's code works,
part by part, with a live Frank you can take apart. I asked for it because I wanted to
understand the code I was turning in, not just have it work. The notes are what I took
away from it as I went, with a clarification underneath when I had something slightly
off.
